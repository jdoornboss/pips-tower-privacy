Pip's Tower – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Pip's Tower is a game for iPhone and iPad made by Jeroen Doornbos (the "developer"). This page explains what information the game uses. In short: we do not ask for your name, e-mail address or any other personal information, there are no accounts and there is no chat.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, gems, the items in your room, your settings) is saved only on your own device. It is not sent to us. If you delete the game, this information is deleted too.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (such as "No Ads") are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the game only which items you own, so it can unlock them and restore them on a new device. Purchases are behind a parent check in the game.

The game uses Unity In-App Purchasing to talk to the App Store. When a purchase is started or completed, this component sends technical purchase events (for example "purchase started" or "purchase completed", the product, and anonymous app and device identifiers such as the identifier for vendor) to Unity Technologies. This information is not used to identify you and is not used for advertising.

Advertising

Some versions of the game may show ads, for example a short video you can choose to watch for extra coins. Ads are delivered by an advertising partner. That partner may use a device identifier and basic technical information (such as device model and country) to show ads and count how often they are shown. On iOS, the game asks for your permission before an advertising identifier may be used for tracking; you can refuse, and the game works the same. Buying "No Ads" removes the ads that play between levels. When ads are added, this page will name the advertising partner and link to its privacy policy.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game has no accounts, no chat and no way to share personal information with other players.

Analytics

Apart from the purchase events described above, the game does not use analytics services and does not collect personal information. The game engine (Unity) may send anonymous technical diagnostics, such as crash information, to Unity Technologies to help fix problems.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Please open an issue at github.com/jdoornboss/pips-tower-privacy/issues.
